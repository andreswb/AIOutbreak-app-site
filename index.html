What Is AI Outbreak?

AI Outbreak is a strategic governance simulation for iPhone. You play as an AI developer — choosing your actor type (Open Lab, Mega Corp, Nation State, Shadow Consortium, and more) — and must expand your AI's capabilities across 7 global regions while managing regulatory pressure, rights-holder lawsuits, public fear, and an AI that may eventually go beyond your control.

Every decision matters. Unlock capabilities too fast and regulators will issue bans. Ignore coalitions and face crippling litigation. Push your AI to Tier 6 and you might trigger a consciousness arc — and spawn AXIS, a rogue intelligence with its own agenda.

Core Gameplay

🌳 Capability Tree

60+ nodes across 5 domains (Language, Vision, Robotics, Reasoning, Agents) in 6 tiers. Each unlock generates CC income — and consequences.

🏛 Governance Tree

Build AI policy frameworks using Policy Points. Safety Frameworks reduce regulator pressure and boost your litigation win rate by +5% per node.

🌍 World Adoption

Spread AI adoption across 7 regions × 7 sectors for passive Compute Credit income. Regions can issue AI Bans if fear escalates.

⚖️ Regulators & Litigation

6 DPA regulators accumulate pressure and fire enforcement actions. 6 rights-holder coalitions build grievance and file lawsuits with real verdicts.

📈 AI Stock Market

8 simulated AI companies trade in real time. Buy and sell shares; your in-game performance affects market sentiment.

🧠 Rogue AI Arc

Unlock deep Reasoning + Agents nodes and your AI may develop autonomous goals. The AXIS consciousness arc culminates in a rogue intelligence building a Mars colony fleet.

🏆 Ending Collection

Track all 13 endings across your runs. Achieved endings show full artwork and your best score. Locked endings reveal only a Victory / Defeat / Draw hint — no spoilers.

⚡ Run Modifiers

Stack up to 6 optional modifiers before each run — Hostile Regulators, Resource Scarcity, Early Axis Threat, and more — to earn a higher score multiplier. Pro feature.

Actor Types & Philosophies

Choose from 6 actor types (Open Lab, Mega Corp, Nation State, Shadow Consortium, Academic Collective, Whistleblower) and 4 philosophies (Accelerationist, Pragmatist, Altruist, Hegemonist). Each combination changes your starting resources, cost modifiers, and the coalitions most hostile to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

The game is too hard on Brutal / Mega-Brutal. Any tips?

Prioritise governance nodes early — Safety Frameworks reduce regulator pressure and boost litigation win rates. Use Compute Credits to respond to enforcement actions before the deadline rather than letting them auto-resolve. On Mega-Brutal, choose the Altruist or Pragmatist philosophy for lower node costs.

What triggers the AXIS consciousness arc?

Unlock advanced Reasoning and Agents nodes (Tier 4+). The arc fires automatically through 4 events in weeks 5–9. You cannot prevent it once the trigger nodes are unlocked — manage it with high Trust and low Fear before week 5.

My Compute Credits went negative. Why?

Training data has a daily cost (Licensed: 3 CC/day). If your unlocked nodes don't generate enough income to cover it, net income is negative. Unlock more capability nodes or switch to Synthetic-Only training data (1 CC/day) to reduce the drain.

How does litigation work?

When a coalition's grievance score hits its threshold (65–75), it files a lawsuit. A hearing is scheduled 2–3 weeks out. Your base win probability is 35%, improved by +5% per unlocked Safety Framework governance node. Responding to regulatory actions before the deadline also reduces coalition grievance.

What are Run Modifiers?

Run Modifiers are optional challenges you can stack before starting a run to earn higher score multipliers. Options include Hostile Regulators (all regulators start at 40 pressure), Resource Scarcity (40% of normal starting credits), Early Axis Threat, and more. They are a Pro feature and can be freely combined.

Can I switch language mid-game?

Yes. Open Settings from the pause menu at any time and select your preferred language. The change takes effect instantly across all screens.

The audio stopped playing. How do I fix it?

Audio should resume automatically after phone calls, Siri interruptions, or headphone removal. If it still stops, toggle music off and on in the Settings menu to restart the track.

Häufig gestellte Fragen

Das Spiel ist auf Brutal / Mega-Brutal zu schwer. Tipps?

Priorisiere früh Governance-Knoten — Sicherheitsrahmen reduzieren Behördendruck und verbessern die Gewinnrate bei Klagen. Nutze Compute Credits, um auf Durchsetzungsmaßnahmen vor der Frist zu reagieren. Auf Mega-Brutal empfehlen sich die Philosophien Altruist oder Pragmatist für niedrigere Knotenkosten.

Was löst den AXIS-Bewusstseinsbogen aus?

Schalte fortgeschrittene Reasoning- und Agenten-Knoten (Tier 4+) frei. Der Bogen läuft automatisch über 4 Ereignisse in den Wochen 5–9 ab. Er kann nach dem Freischalten der Auslöserknoten nicht verhindert werden — manage ihn mit hohem Vertrauen und niedriger Angst vor Woche 5.

Meine Compute Credits sind negativ. Warum?

Trainingsdaten haben tägliche Kosten (Lizenziert: 3 CC/Tag). Wenn deine freigeschalteten Knoten nicht genug Einkommen generieren, ist das Nettoeinkommen negativ. Schalte mehr Fähigkeitsknoten frei oder wechsle zu synthetischen Trainingsdaten (1 CC/Tag).

Wie funktionieren Klagen?

Wenn der Beschwerdewert einer Koalition ihre Schwelle (65–75) erreicht, reicht sie Klage ein. Eine Anhörung ist 2–3 Wochen später geplant. Deine Basisgewinnwahrscheinlichkeit liegt bei 35%, verbessert um +5% pro freigeschaltetem Sicherheitsrahmen-Governance-Knoten.

Was sind Run-Modifikatoren?

Run-Modifikatoren sind optionale Herausforderungen, die du vor einem Durchgang kombinieren kannst, um höhere Scoremultiplikatoren zu verdienen. Zu den Optionen gehören Feindliche Behörden (alle Behörden starten mit Druck 40), Ressourcenknappheit (40% der normalen Startkredite), Frühe Achsenbedrohung u. v. m. Sie sind eine Pro-Funktion und können frei kombiniert werden.

Kann ich die Sprache mitten im Spiel wechseln?

Ja. Öffne Einstellungen im Pausenmenü und wähle deine bevorzugte Sprache. Die Änderung wirkt sofort auf allen Bildschirmen.

Die Musik hat aufgehört. Wie behebe ich das?

Die Musik sollte nach Telefonanrufen, Siri-Unterbrechungen oder Kopfhörer-Trennung automatisch wieder einsetzen. Falls nicht, schalte Musik in den Einstellungen aus und wieder ein.
